Every hill in England, measured for its view

This site searched the whole of England — 52 million terrain cells — for places with the geometry of a great panorama: a steep drop at your feet, a wide unobstructed horizon, long sightlines over lower country, and a varied visible landscape. No ratings, no likes — only physics: 46,369 candidate spots ranked by objective view potential.
