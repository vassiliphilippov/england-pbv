Which view is more beautiful?

The score on this site is pure geometry — it has never been told what people actually like. Help fix that: two real photographs of English views, taken from known spots. Pick the one you find more beautiful. Every vote sharpens the comparison between the formula and human taste.

356 photographs, all free-licensed with camera location. Votes are stored only in your browser — use the export button to contribute them.

Your votes stay on this device

Nothing is uploaded. When you have voted a while, export the file and send it to the project (e.g. attach it to a GitHub issue) — it will be used to fit a model of human preference over the objective metrics, and to re-weight the view-potential formula.
