Does it actually work?

The ranking was tested against 60 famous, widely-celebrated English viewpoints (it should rank them highly) and 20 adversarial negative controls — forested summits, flat fens, valley floors directly below famous viewpoints, plateau tops with altitude but no prospect (it should rank them low). Every positive's photo was visually checked against the target view type. Human judgements were used only to test the ranking, never to build it.

51/60 famous viewpoints pass (top-25% nationally or top-10% of their region) · 17/20 negative controls pass (bottom half nationally).
